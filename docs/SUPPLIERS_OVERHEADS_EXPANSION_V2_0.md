# Suppliers & Overheads Expansion v2.0

Executable tests: SUP-01 through SUP-06.

New evidence objects: supplier_master, purchase_transaction, overhead_transaction.

Economic controls:
- spend concentration is descriptive, not proof of overspend;
- PPV is historical/mechanical impact, not automatic recoverable opportunity;
- dependency is exposure, not expected loss;
- cost drift is not automatically waste;
- duplicate-looking spend is a forensic case, not proof of error;
- supplier/overhead candidates remain unquantified until an evidenced mechanism and addressability/recovery basis exist.
