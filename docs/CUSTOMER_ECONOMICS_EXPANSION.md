# Customer Economics Expansion — v1.6

Status: CUS-01 through CUS-07 executable.

Guardrails:
1. Revenue scale is not customer profitability.
2. Contribution 0 is not Contribution after Cost-to-Serve.
3. CTS is never invented when source evidence is absent.
4. Observed absence in a comparison window is a churn/lapse signal, subject to cadence/contract context.
5. Observed revenue retention is not labelled contractual GRR without contract evidence.
6. AR ageing snapshot evidence does not establish historical payment speed or collectability.
7. Concentration is exposure, not expected loss or probability of customer failure.
8. Pareto distribution is descriptive and does not imply long-tail customers should be exited.
9. Customer movement is not causal explanation.
10. No Customer diagnostic creates an opportunity value directly.
