# v2.39 Level-3 Synthetic Business Qualification

Purpose: exercise the complete 56-diagnostic estate against a deliberately rich synthetic manufacturing evidence set rather than increasing the diagnostic count.

The case combines transaction-level customer/product sales and margin evidence with workforce/capacity, supplier purchasing and overheads, AR ageing, working-capital primitives, budget/actual/forecast vintages, KPI observations, and risk/control evidence.

This is **synthetic qualification evidence, not real-SME validation**. CRM/pipeline remains a named architectural data domain but the current 56-test registry contains no standalone CRM diagnostic; CRM evidence is therefore not falsely counted as an additional diagnostic. Full D15 product qualification remains an open gate until pipeline objects/methods are implemented or explicitly mapped into existing forward-looking commercial tests.

Guardrails remain unchanged: anomalies are not fraud, exposure is not expected loss, cash release is not profit, capacity is not saving, and scenarios are not predictions.
