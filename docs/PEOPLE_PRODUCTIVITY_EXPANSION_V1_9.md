# Profit Doctor v1.9 — People & Productivity Expansion

Executable tests: PEO-01 through PEO-05.

## D11 workforce model
`workforce_snapshot` stores employee-level snapshot evidence: FTE, department, role, base salary, employer on-cost, commission, bonus, other people cost, practical capacity and utilised hours. `commission_plan` stores evidenced incentive-plan structure separately from paid variable compensation.

## Economic guardrails
- Total People Cost includes evidenced salary, employer on-cost, commission, bonus and other people cost.
- Revenue / Contribution per FTE are ratios, not proof of individual productivity or causality.
- Department and role comparisons do not rank unlike functions without comparable output drivers.
- Practical capacity is distinct from theoretical paid hours.
- Unused capacity is measured in hours, not pounds.
- Capacity released has £0 financial benefit until cost is removed, evidenced future hiring/contractor/overtime is avoided, or additional profitable work is absorbed.
- Commission-plan structure and commission spend do not prove incentive effectiveness or incremental revenue causality.

## Progressive degradation
People-cost analysis can run from D11 workforce/payroll evidence. Output-per-FTE additionally requires commercial output evidence. Utilisation refuses to run without practical-capacity and utilised-hours evidence.
