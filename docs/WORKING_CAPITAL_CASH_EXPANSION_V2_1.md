# Working Capital & Cash Expansion — v2.1

Executable registry: WC-01 through WC-07.

## Economic rules enforced
- Cash release is not profit.
- Collecting recognised revenue is not incremental profit.
- Profit-to-cash manifestation must not be counted as a second benefit.
- Inventory value/DIO does not establish safely releasable cash.
- DPO/payables do not establish that delaying suppliers is economically safe.
- Available Cash is not Available Liquidity without documented facility headroom.
- Exposure/balance is not Addressable or Expected cash release.
- Cash opportunity candidates remain unquantified until cause, mechanism, timing and guardrails are evidenced.

## Tests
Six dedicated adversarial tests cover registry completeness, supplier-payment restraint, inventory-release restraint, liquidity semantics, unquantified release candidates, and profit/cash anti-double-counting.
