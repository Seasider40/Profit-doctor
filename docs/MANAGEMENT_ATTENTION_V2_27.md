# Profit Doctor v2.27 — Management Attention / FD Output Qualification

## Purpose
Compress a large evidence set into a small, traceable management agenda without converting size into priority, inventing causality, or manufacturing opportunity value.

## Policy
- Target 3–7 distinct management themes when the evidence genuinely supports them; never pad the agenda.
- Cluster related signals into themes while retaining underlying signal evidence.
- Priority combines materiality, corroboration, decision blockage and evidence reliability. Raw £ magnitude is not a standalone ranking rule.
- Reconciliation residuals remain control exceptions, not assumed losses.
- Capacity anomalies are either operational evidence or a data-quality issue; implausible source data is refused rather than repaired silently.
- Attention selection creates no monetised opportunity and no causal conclusion.
