# v2.28 Management Action & Opportunity Qualification

Adds an action layer and Profit & Cash Opportunity Register to the v2.27 FD attention agenda.

Rules: management priority does not equal opportunity; reconciliation residual does not equal loss/cash; capacity released does not equal saving; theoretical does not equal addressable; addressable/expected economics require mechanism and recovery-envelope evidence. The register therefore carries evidence requirements and actions even where monetary opportunity remains deliberately unquantified.
