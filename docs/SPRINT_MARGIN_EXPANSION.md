# Gross Margin & Leakage Expansion — v1.5

Executable tests: GM-01 through GM-07.

Key controls:
- Comparable contribution margin is distinct from revenue and GP pounds.
- Customer/product margin-rate effects are historical impacts, not automatic opportunities.
- Leakage signals require an evidenced comparable product baseline and remain investigative until cause/addressability are established.
- Purchase-cost inflation recovery compares unit cost and unit selling price on comparable products; recovery is mechanically capped at cost pressure.
- Negative/abnormal transaction margins are forensic anomalies, not proof of error, misconduct, leakage or recoverable saving.
- All outputs retain dataset-version lineage and flow into the existing reasoning/economic architecture.
